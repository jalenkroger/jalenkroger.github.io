Rules and structure
3px red — under the masthead only, once per page
2px ink — opens a section or a table
1px rule — between rows and columns
Nothing is rounded. Every corner on every screen is square — boxes, buttons, fields, status marks, flaps. Newspapers are made of rules and columns, and a single 6px radius would give the whole thing away.
No shadows, no fills. Depth comes from rules and whitespace. A card is a hairline box on the sheet, not a floating panel.

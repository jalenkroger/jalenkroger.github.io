Data reflects University of Nebraska system personnel salary records (UNL, UNL–IANR/Nebraska Extension, UNO, UNK, UNMC, NCTA, and NU System Administration), as provided in the source spreadsheet. Figures shown are the listed base salary and full-time-equivalent (FTE) status for each position; employees appointed at less than 1.0 FTE (e.g. part-time, adjunct, or partial-year roles) will show a lower salary than an equivalent full-time position. A small number of rows in the source data represent unfilled/placeholder budget lines rather than named individuals. This is an independent project and is not an official University of Nebraska system website.
